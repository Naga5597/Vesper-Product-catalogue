Technical overview

Vesher's NEPHROCARE KIdhey feshers The urinary system ofthe birds filters,secretes, absorbs, stores and eliminates urine periodically,the urinarysystem consistof paired trilobed kidneys, one on each side of the ventral surface ofthe vertebral column. Ureters carry the urinary waste to the urodeum ofthecloaca.Avian kidneysaredivided into unitscalled lobules. Each lobule has acortex (outer area) and medulla (or medullary cone). In terms of volume,the avian kidney is primarily cortex(71-81%). plus a relatively small medulla (range 5 15%)& blood vessels larger than capillaries (range10-13%). The functional unit of the kidney is the nephron. Avian kidneys have two kinds of nephrons.

A reptilian-type, with no loops of Henle are located in the cortex,and a mammalian-type with long or intermediate length loops, are located in the medulla. In birds, only a smallpercentage of nephrons (15-25%) contain a loop of Henle (i.e. loopednephrons). Nephrons filter the blood plasma to eliminate waste products, but, in doing so, must not lose needed materials (like glucose) or too much water. Blood enters nephrons via smallarteries called afferent arterioles. This blood enters the glomerulus (a collection of capillaries) under high pressure and filters' through the walls of the capillaries and the walls ofa surrounding structure called a capsule.

Plasma is filtered from the glomerular capillaries into the glomerular capsule. Filtrate then travels through the tubules and loop of Henle before entering the collecting duct. The filtrate that moves from the glomerular capsule into the proximal tubules is basically plasma without protein (the protein molecules are toolarge). That filtrate, therefore, contains lots of important substances. In the proximal convoluted tubules,those needed substances such as vitamins and glucose are reabsorbed into the blood. Gout is a common metabolic disorder that results in abnormal accumulation of urates in kidneys and several otherorgans ofthe birds body.

Gout is a generic term for a disease of poultry called toxic nephritis. Nephritis is the inflammation ofthe idneys. The toxicpart is usually caused by uric acid. Uric acid isthe biproductofthe metabolism ofnitrogen from ingested feed. The Uric Acid is produced mainly in the liver, and the Uric Acid is eliminated via the kidneys. Gout usually present itself in two different forms. The visceral form develops urate deposits on any of the internal organs. The articular form forms urates in the joints causing severe swelling of the joints.

Numerous factors are responsible forthis condition like imbalance of calcium and phosphorus, feeding high calcium diets to chicks and growers, urea adulteration,. mycotoxins (Aflatoxins, Ochratoxins and Oosporin), high protein levels in feed, poor quality proteins, use of rock phosphate, bacterialand viral infections, Zinc and other heavy metal toxicity, use of certain antibiotics (Gentamycin, Sulfa drugs), certain Genetic problems etc., anyone or a combination of the above mentioned can damage the kidneys, reducing theirfunction leading toGout HPSorAscites.
