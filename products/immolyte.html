Clinical data shows that Immolyte is the clearchoice in augmenting cell mediated immunity as well as antibody titres. Immolyte cuts down the incidence of diseases, reduces vaccination failures, improves overall performance and reduces mortality.

Immolyte enhances the efficacy of antibiotics and thereby reduces the dosage and treatment period Todaythe marketexhibits many"immunostimulant" products. Mostsuch productsare either mixtures of certain vitamins and minerals or so called herbal products containing "Amla" (Gooseberries). It is truethatcertain vitamins and minerals like VitaminE, Vitamin C, Selenium etc., (andhenceAmla, containingVitamin C) can help improve the immunity. But, the magnitude of such an improvement is limited due to the fact that these nutrients are not always deficient and are provided in required quantities in most poultry rations oftoday.

Biologically speaking, giving more ofthese nutrients will notsimply increase the immunity correspondingly. IMMOLYTE Fitness of the immune system depends on three important factors viz.,Water-electrolyte balance (generally referred to as homeostasis), nutritional fulfilment (expecially with respect to Vitamin E, Vitamin C, Se etc.) and immune stimulants (antigens/antigenic associates capable of exciting / arousing the immune system). While most products in themarket provide for the first two factors, theygenerallylack the immunestimulantfactorwhich, in fact,is the most important componentofan immunopotentiatormeantfor poultry use.

Immolyte contains Glycine (an osmolyte) and electrolytes which maintain the water electrolyte balanceand preserve homestasis. Vitamins E and C, andSelenium, contributeto a better cellular health and general immunity. Immolyte also contains unique immune stimulation components like the aminonitrogen (antigenic protein) and specific polysaccharide. Aminonitrogen in Immolyte (antigenic protein)produces non-specific antibodies, andpolysaccharidesactasasource oflymphokines improving theCMI. Bacterial loadonthebird is broughtdown bythecombnedeffects ofLactoperoxidasesystem and yeast nucleins. The overall effect of Immolyte, keeps the immune system prepared to react spontaneously and effectively.
