Selective Biotechnological Feed Acidifier

Organic acids for health and performance

Organic acids are used globally by the feed industry for management of gut microbiology, improved performance and health. The brochure states that their use supports better nutrient utilization and use of enzymes like phytase, better body weights, improved feed conversion and reduced colonization of the gut by pathogens, thereby reducing disease and disturbance in the flock.

Further context

The brochure discusses public awareness of cross-resistance of pathogens to antibiotics and states that propionic acid has been used for more than 30 years to reduce bacterial growth and mould in feedstuffs. It cites health and performance effects for formic, fumaric, citric and lactic acids and their salts, including feed conversion, daily gain, diarrhea incidence, feed cost and time to market.

Organic acid definition

The brochure defines organic acids as a broad class of compounds used in fundamental metabolic processes, sharing water solubility, acidity and ninhydrin-negativity; it generally includes carboxylic acids with or without keto or hydroxyl groups but excludes most amino acids.

Post-table technical note

Amino nitrogen and reactive protein stimulate macrophage activation and improve cell-mediated immunity in the birds. LPO (Lactoperoxidase) is bacteriostatic and bactericidal, so it supports probiotics. Bactocare OA+ provides a maximized comprehensive probiotic activity to competitively inhibit, antagonize and exclude pathogens for improved production and profit, thereby reducing dependence on AGP (Antibiotic Growth Promoters).

Product information

Composition

ORGANIC ACIDS (Formic acid, Sorbic acid, Propionic Acid, Acetic acid, Ascorbic acid and Lactic acid), Ammonium propionate, Ammonium Formate along with fatty acids, yeast extract, Amino Nitrogen, LPO, reactive Proteins in a fortified base.

Indications / Uses

  • Reduces the gut pH.
  • Reduces multiplication of pathogenic gram-negative microorganisms and fungus.
  • Increases tissue response and improves nutrient utilization.
  • Reduces disease incidences.
  • The brochure positions it as an alternative to antibiotic growth promoters.
  • Reduces duration and cost of treatment.

Usage Instructions

Regular Use: 250 gm–500 gm/ton of feed. During Stress: 2 kg/ton of feed. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

1 kg × 25 packets in a bag.
