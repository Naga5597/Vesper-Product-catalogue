TOXOL

Nutritional Liver & Kidney Supplement

Product information and technical overview.

Liver and kidney role

The liver is the most metabolically active organ of the chicken and the site of many nutrient-assimilation, drug-catabolism and toxin-detoxification reactions. The kidneys are the main organs for excretion of waste and metabolized products.

Fat accumulation and metabolic stress

Many liver disorders result in fat accumulation. Increasing accumulation reduces liver-cell metabolic functions and can affect growth, production and detoxification. Clinical signs may appear only after about 75% of the liver is damaged, highlighting the importance of accelerating removal of lipid molecules from liver cells.

Lipid metabolism and Toxol

Toxol supports lipid metabolism through lipotropic factors, lipo-acceptor protein, lipoprotein lipase and pathways involving triglycerides, phospholipids, sphingolipids, ketone bodies, cholesterol and steroids.

Commercial broiler trial data

A 36-day commercial broiler comparison evaluated control and Toxol treatment groups. Body weights increased with Toxol dosage, FCR improved compared with the control group and mortality was reduced in treatment groups. The displayed data include body-weight, FCR, mortality and feed-consumption values.

Product information

Composition

Toxol Feed Premix: Choline Chloride, Methionine, Inositol, Vitamin B12, base fortified with Predigested Protein and Aminonitrogen. Toxol Liquid: each 10 ml contains Tricholine Citrate 100 mg, Methionine 10 mg, Liver extract 50 mg, Protein Hydrolysate 50 mg, Inositol 2 mg, Vitamin B12 0.33 mcg, base fortified with Predigested Protein and Aminonitrogen.

Indications / Uses

  • Improves Liver & Kidney Health.
  • Protect from damaging effect of Mycotoxins.
  • Improves Nutrient Utilization.

Toxol
Liquid: 5–15 ml/100 birds. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.
