NEPHROCARE

Renal support for poultry

Product information and technical overview.

Avian urinary system

The urinary system filters, secretes, absorbs, stores and eliminates urine. Poultry have paired trilobed kidneys, ureters, renal lobules, cortex and medulla, with the nephron serving as the functional unit. Reptilian-type and mammalian-type nephrons contribute to the filtering and reabsorption process.

Gout in poultry

Gout involves abnormal accumulation of urates in the kidneys and other organs. Visceral and articular forms can occur. Contributing factors include calcium/phosphorus imbalance, high-calcium diets in chicks and growers, urea adulteration, mycotoxins, high protein levels, poor-quality proteins, rock phosphate, bacterial or viral infections, zinc and heavy-metal toxicity, certain antibiotics and genetic problems.

Nephrocare concept

Nephrocare contains nephromodulator substances, sodium fluoride, butyric acid and aminonitrogen in a fortified base. Sodium fluoride acts as a kidney stimulant, butyric acid helps reduce the chances of kidney damage by pathogens, and nephromodulator substances support physiological renal functioning and rejuvenation.

Reported outcomes

Improved renal function can support detoxification, endogenous Vitamin C generation, reabsorption of nutrients and electrolytes, and haemopoiesis. Nephrocare helps reduce mortality associated with gout/ascites and supports renal-tissue rejuvenation.

Product information

Indications / Uses
