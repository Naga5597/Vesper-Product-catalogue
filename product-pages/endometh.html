ENDOMETH

Look beyond synthetic methionine

Product information and technical overview.

Enterokinase and methionine reconversion

Two mechanisms support Endometh: enterokinase stimulation and methionine reconversion. Methionine is the first limiting amino acid in poultry diets, and other amino acids are utilized only to the extent of available methionine.

Supplementing synthetic methionine

Calculated methionine from feed ingredients may vary with ingredient quality. Even when synthetic methionine is supplemented, a deficit may remain if the expected dietary contribution is not realized.

Maximizing methionine utilization

Supplying protease may help only to a certain extent. A greater enterokinase response to food can improve native protease activity. Maximizing conversion of trypsinogen to trypsin can improve protein digestibility and reduce the requirement for trypsinogen production, which has a high sulphur-amino-acid content.

Enhanced methionine reconversion

When the body requires methyl groups, methionine is converted to homocystine. Homocystine can be reconverted to methionine in the presence of a labile methyl donor, but this process may be limited by inadequate activation of the enzyme system. Aminonitrogen in Endometh activates the corresponding enzyme system and promotes reconversion of homocystine into methionine.

Broiler trial reported

A commercial broiler trial used 580 day-old chicks divided into control and Endometh groups for 42 days. The trial measured weekly body weight, feed intake, FCR and antibody titres. The Endometh group recorded an average body weight 5 g higher, 153 g less feed consumed per bird, an FCR of 2.02, similar immunity, and an additional return of Rs. 1260 per 1000 broilers compared with DL-methionine under the stated trial assumptions. The comparison also used stated DL-methionine and Endometh price assumptions and feed-cost calculations.

Product information

Composition

Methyl donors and Aminonitrogen in a base enriched with Natural enhancers of Enterokinase Activity.

Indications / Uses

  • To enhance the methionine value of feedstuffs.

Usage Instructions

In synthetic methionine supplemented diets: replace total synthetic methionine content with equal quantity of Endometh. In non-supplemented diets: add 100–150 gm of Endometh for better feed utilization, productivity and FCR.

Presentation

20 Kg bags.
