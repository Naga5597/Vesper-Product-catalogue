Benefits Of Enterokinase Stimulation

Methionine being the first limiting amino acid in poultry diets, all the other amino acids present in the dietary protein will be utilized only to the extent of the available methionine. Remaining amino acids will be deaminated and used forenergyprocess,whichisrelativelyexpensive. To avoid this wasteful protein metabolism and to turn it into a benefit there are two ways. One, increase the available methionine content by supplementing crystalline methionine. Two, improvetheoverallavailability ofnaturaldietary methioninebyanintegratedapproach.

Approach 1- Supplementing Synthetic Methionine: While supplementing synthetic methionine, we are considering that a calculated proportion of methionine is coming from the feed ingredients. However, due to variation in thequality of ingredients, most of the times we may not get the amount of methionine we expected from the dietary source, even though it may be physically present. Thus, even though we are supplementing a high level of synthetic methionine, there might be still a deficit ofmethionine, which brings downtheperformance of thebirdsgreatly. Approach 2 -Maximizing Methionine Utilization: Supplying protease, though, could help to certain extent the benefit is only marginal.

Eliciting a greater enterokinase response to the presence of food thereby improving the native protease activity is a possibility which appears to be significantly beneficial. In addition, maximizing the conversion oftrypsinogen to trypsin helps in twoways(1) better proteindigestibility leading to reducedrequirement for methionine supply and (2) Reduced requirement for Trypsinogen production (Trypsinogen has a very high sulphur amino acid content and requireshigheramounts of methionineforitssynthesis). In both the above situations, Vesper's ENDOMETH helps the bird's system to spare valuable methionine in poultry diets.

Used in replacement of the crystalline aminoacid methionine, it can bring about better utilization of methionine, protein and hence all the nutrients for a better productivity and FCR. Enhanced MethionineReconversion Whenever the body requires methyl groups, methionine isstripped ofa methyl group and in the process it is converted into Homocystine. This homocystine caneither beconverted intocystine, a not-so-essential aminoacid as compared to methionine, or it can be reconverted into methionine in the presence ofa labilemethyldonordonatingmethylgroup.

Many atimes,even in thepresence ofmethyl groups, thereconversionofhomocystine to methionine may not take place due to lack of activation of the proper enzyme system involved. Aminonitrogen in Endometh activates the coresponding enzyme system and thereby promotes the reconversion of homocystine into methionine. Thus, Endometh can reducetherequirementofSynthetic methionine in poultrydiets. Usedin total replacementofthecrystalline amino acid methionine, it canbring about betterutilization ofmethionine, protein and hence allthe nutrients for a betterproductivityandFCR.

Trial on the Efficacy of ENDOMETH to replace DL-methionine in broiler diets A trial was conducted in commercial broilers to evaluate the efficacy of Endometh to replaceDL-methionine in broilerdictsand itseconomical impact. Theexperiment consisted ofS80 day-old chicks divided into two groups and reared on common starter and finisher broiler diets, supplemented with either DL-methionine (Control) orcquivalent proportion ofEndometh (Endometh).

The birds were reared for 42 days under common conditions- during whichobservations were made. The birds were observed weekly for body weight and feed intake, and FCR was calculated. At the end ofthe rearing, 10% of birds from each group were tested for antibody titresagainst IBD (by ELISA) and ND (by HI). Finally, observations were compiled and the resultswerecompared. Resultspertainingto body weightand FCR are given in fig. 1. Birds from Endometh group were weighing on an average 5 gms more, and had consumed 153 gms per bird lesser, resulting in a much better FCR (2.02)with out oil. Immunity as indicated by antibody levels was similar for both the groups.

After calculating the Feed cost it was found that Endometh resulted in an additional returns of Rs. 1260/- per 1000 broilers as compared to DL-methionine. The above trial demonstrates the ability of Endometh to bring about a greater reconversion ofmethionine and hence a better nutrient utilization in poultry, especially in broilers. In the absence ofsuch an influence, it is assumed that the pure DL-methionine activitymayonlytry tocompensate the growth requirements by increasing the feed consumption. However, such an increase in feed consumption may not be accompanied by an cquivalent growth rate, thus resulting in a poorerFCR.
