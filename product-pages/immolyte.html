IMMOLYTE

The CHOICE Immunopotentiator

Product information and technical overview.

Immune-system concept

Three important factors support immune-system fitness: water-electrolyte balance (homeostasis), nutritional fulfilment, especially Vitamin E, Vitamin C and selenium, and immune stimulants such as antigens/antigenic associates capable of exciting the immune system.

Immolyte formulation concept

Immolyte contains glycine (an osmolyte) and electrolytes to maintain water-electrolyte balance and preserve homeostasis. Vitamins E and C and selenium contribute to cellular health and general immunity. It also contains aminonitrogen (antigenic protein) and a specific polysaccharide. Aminonitrogen produces non-specific antibodies, while polysaccharides act as a source of lymphokines that improve CMI.

Microbial and immune support

The combined effects of the lactoperoxidase system and yeast nucleins help reduce bacterial load and keep the immune system prepared to react spontaneously and effectively.

Clinical and product performance

Reported clinical data indicate improvements in cell-mediated immunity and antibody titres, reduced disease incidence and vaccination failures, improved performance and reduced mortality, and enhanced antibiotic efficacy.

Product information

Composition

Glycine, Vitamin E, Selenium, Vitamin C, Sodium Chloride, Potassium Chloride, Sodium Citrate, Yeast nucleins, Aminonitrogen (Antigenic protein) with Lactoperoxidase and polysaccharides in a fortified base.

Indications / Uses

  • Improves the antibody titers.
  • Reduces vaccination failures.
  • Reduces the incidence of disease through improved CMI.
  • Enhances productivity by reducing the incidence of subclinical infections.

Usage Instructions

Immolyte
Liquid: Chicks, Growers and Broilers: 5 ml per liter. Layers: 10 ml per liter.

Presentation

Liquid: 100 ml, 500 ml bottle and 5 Ltrs Jar.
Special instructions: use regularly during the first 2 weeks of chick’s life; thereafter, use 3 days before and after vaccinations, deworming or anticipated stress. Usage during outbreaks and convalescence is advised.
