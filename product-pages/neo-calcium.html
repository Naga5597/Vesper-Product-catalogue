Technical overview

Neocalcium |Feed Premix shellwith a differern The saying "For every complex problem there is a simple solution." may be true in many general cases but proved to be wrong in particular cases of mineral nutrition of birds. Mineral interrelationships are very complex. In view of the different purposes for which the birds are being reared, and with different ages and stages of production mineral requirements do vary a lot. Especialy important are the vital macrominerals, Calcium and Phosphorous.

With so many calcium and phosphorous supplements being used,apart from the mineral mix,shellgrit, DCP et., the poultry industry is, naturally, expected to have no problems such as thin shelled eggs, broken eggs, shellless eggs, bone breakage problems, osteomalacea, rickets, etc. Unfortunately, the problems are quite common. This clearly shows that something is missing in Calcium - Phosphorous nutrition and the supplemented calcium and phosphorous are not being utilised properly . Importance of Calcium and Phosphorous Calcium is essential for proper bone growth in growing animals, bone maintenance in adults and shell production in laying birds.

Apart from this Calcium is also necessary for blood clotting proper neuromuscular transmission contraction of and musle fibres, Absorption of Calcium from the digestive tract is adversely affected due to parasiti infestations,presence of oxalates and phytates in feed, higher levels of other cations such aslron Magnesium, Aluminium etc.., and improper ratio of Calcium and Phosphorous. Calcium deficiency leads to dropin egg production and incidence of thin shelled and shelless eggs in layers and mavleard to crippling, lameness and death.

The Calcium deficient broilers willnot be able to bear their own hed weioht ontheir limbs due to osteoarthritis.This may lead to starvation, loss in body weight gain and sometimes, death. Also bone breakage is more during tránsport in Calcium deficient birds resultinain carcass rejection and economic losses. Phosphorous has a very vital role to play in the energy metabolism of the body cells apart from t'e bone forming functions.

Phosphorous is an integral part of various structural and functionalcomponents of the body such as cell membranes, membranes of organelle, nucleic acids, ATP molecules ote Phosphorous, as Cyclic AMP, functions as secondary messenger in intracelular communication and as phosphate buffer, maintains the homeostasis of the body fluids. Deficiency of Phosphorous resultsin retarded growth and rickets. Improper Calcium-Phosphorous ratio upsets the phosphorous absorption/ metabolism and creates a deficiency. Phytate bound Phosphorous is not available to the birds asthev lack the enzyme Phytase. Magnesium is a vital divalent cation necessary for the birds.

Both Calcium and Magnesium being divalent cations, there is a competition between them for absorption. Hence higherlevels of Calciumin the ciet increase the requirement of Magnesium. Neglecting this may possibly result in marginal Magnesium deficiency, stunted growth and lethargy in chicks anddrop in egg production in laying birds. Neocalcium granules contain Calcium and Phosphorous in the right ratio of 2:1, for proper absorption. metabolism and utilization by the bird.

Neocalcium granules contain Magnesium in order to overcome the competitive effects of Calcium indiet and to maintain right body levels of Calcium,Phosphorous and Magnesium for proper bone growth, egg production and shell thickness. The base material fortified with peptide moiety aids in better absorption of Calcium from the digestive tract and proper mobilization from medullary bones during.the shell formation process for better egg shells.
