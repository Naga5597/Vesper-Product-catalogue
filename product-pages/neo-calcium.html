NEOCALCIUM

Feed Premix — The shell with a difference

Product information and technical overview.

Mineral nutrition

Mineral interrelationships are complex, and calcium and phosphorus requirements vary with purpose, age and production stage. Thin-shelled, broken and shellless eggs, bone breakage, osteomalacia and rickets can persist despite calcium/phosphorus supplementation.

Calcium

Calcium is essential for proper bone growth in growing animals, bone maintenance in adults and shell production in laying birds. It is also necessary for blood clotting, neuromuscular transmission and muscle-fibre contraction. Calcium absorption can be adversely affected by parasitic infestations, oxalates, phytates, higher levels of other cations and an improper calcium-phosphorus ratio.

Phosphorus

Phosphorus plays roles in energy metabolism and in structural and functional components including cell membranes, nucleic acids and ATP. Phosphorus deficiency can result in retarded growth and rickets, while phytate-bound phosphorus is not available to birds lacking phytase.

Magnesium and formulation rationale

Magnesium is a vital divalent cation. Because calcium and magnesium compete for absorption, higher dietary calcium increases magnesium requirement. Neocalcium granules contain calcium and phosphorus in a 2:1 ratio and magnesium, while the fortified base with peptide moiety is described as aiding calcium absorption and mobilization from medullary bone during shell formation.

Product information

Composition

Each 5 g contains: Calcium 1250 mg; Phosphorous 625 mg; Magnesium 1.05 mg in a fortified base.

Indications / Uses

  • For thick egg shells, stronger bones, growth and production.
