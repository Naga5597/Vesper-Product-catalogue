ACID-pH

Selective Biotechnological Purifier & Acidifier

Product information and technical overview.

Background

Enteric bacteria and mycotoxins are responsible for a variety of problems in poultry production system today. Commonly used organic acids are formic, acetic, citric, propionic, sorbic and lactic acids. These organic acids and their salts are generally regarded as safe (GRAS) for use in animal and poultry feeds.

How organic acids work

Organic acids and their salts inhibit microbial loads in feed, thereby indirectly maintaining a desirable population of beneficial micro-organisms in the gut. Organic acids positively impact performance in poultry and livestock by keeping pathogenic microorganisms under check. Bactericidal action is exerted by pH that causes dissociation and release of hydrogen, altering bacterial metabolism, depleting energy and causing death.

Gut environment

Organic acids reduce pH of the gut. The acidified digesta reduces production of toxic components by both bacteria and fungus, thereby reducing damage to the epithelial lining of the gut. Lower pH helps increase gastric proteolysis and protein/amino-acid digestibility.

Product information

Composition

Organic Acids (Ascorbic, Citric, Sorbic, Formic, Acetic, Propionic), Ammonium formate, Ammonium propionate along with yeast extract and fatty acid esters in activated base.

Indications / Uses

  • ACID-pH helps to reduce the gut pH and makes it unfavorable for multiplication of disease-causing microorganisms and fungus.
  • ACID-pH improves nutrient utilization, reduces disease incidences and mortality to increase profits.
  • ACID-pH provides an alternative to antibiotic growth promoters, with a low likelihood of contributing to antibiotic resistance and potential to reduce treatment duration and cost.
  • ACID-pH helps improve gut microflora, reducing chances of recurring gut infections, preventing problems of loose dropping and improving litter quality.

Usage Instructions

As Sanitizer: 1 ml per 10 Ltr of drinking water regularly. During Stress: 1–2 ml per Ltr of drinking water for 5–7 days. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian. Note: Do not use ACID-pH 24 hours before, during & after vaccination through drinking water.
