Neuromuscular transmission in poultry

Proper transmission of messages through the nervous system is essential for coordinated movement and effective neuromuscular function. Impaired transmission can contribute to problems such as lameness, vaccination reactions and after-effects of Ranikhet disease.

Neurolyte is formulated to support the nutritional factors involved in neuromuscular transmission and help maintain effective communication between nerves and muscles.

Lameness in birds

Lameness has been a persistent problem in poultry production. The Neurolyte brochure identifies nutritional deficiency as a basic cause and describes several contributing factors, including poor-quality vitamin-mineral mixes, improper mixing, deficiency of a nutrient or its precursor, anticoccidial and antibacterial administration, improper pelleting or extrusion, poor-quality fish meal, unbalanced diets and malabsorption.

Nutritional support therefore needs to consider the wider factors involved in neuromuscular function rather than relying only on calcium, phosphorus and vitamin D3.

Profile of Neurolyte

Neurolyte is a combination of selected vitamins and electrolytes (microminerals), along with amino nitrogen, formulated to address the nutritional factors associated with lameness.

Key areas

  • Lameness due to nutritional deficiencies
  • Vaccination reactions
  • After-effects of Ranikhet disease
  • Support for effective neuromuscular transmission

Neuromuscular function

The formulation is designed to support neurotransmitter release, acetylcholine activity at the motor endplate and balanced transmission across the neuromuscular junction.

Rationale of the composition

Thiamine
Supports acetylcholine release from presynaptic neurons.
Calcium
Necessary for acetylcholine release and skeletal muscle contraction.
Phosphorous
Acts as a phosphate buffer and supports the mechanisms involved in acetylcholine receptor synthesis.
Vitamin D3
Supports absorption and metabolism of calcium and phosphorous.
Riboflavin
Supports axial cylinder strength and myelination of nerves.
Choline
Necessary for synthesis of the neurotransmitter acetylcholine in motoneurons.
Amino Nitrogen
Supports synthesis and development of postsynaptic acetylcholine receptors at the motor endplate.
Zinc & Manganese
Support the functional mechanisms involved in neuromuscular activity and transmission.
Sodium, Potassium & Chloride
Help maintain resting membrane potential and support normal activity of excitable cells.

Key features of Neurolyte

  • Supports neurotransmitter release from the presynaptic neuron.
  • Supports the action of acetylcholine at the motor endplate.
  • Supports synthesis and development of acetylcholine receptors at the motor endplate.
  • Provides balanced transmission across the neuromuscular junction.

Composition

Thiamine, Choline, Zinc, Manganese, Riboflavin, Vitamin D3, Amino nitrogen (peptide moiety), Sodium Citrate, Potassium Chloride with Organic Calcium and Phosphorous in an energy-rich base.

Indications

  • Lameness due to nutritional deficiency
  • After-effects of Ranikhet disease (RD/ND)
  • Vaccination reactions

Usage Instructions

During the first three days of chick's life

1 ml per litre of water

To reduce severity of lameness

1–2 ml per litre of water

Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.
