Product information and technical overview.

Neuromuscular transmission and lameness

Neurolyte supports timely message transmission for effective neuromuscular function in poultry. It is formulated with selected vitamins, electrolytes (microminerals) and amino nitrogen to address nutritional factors associated with lameness.

History of lameness in birds

Lameness has been a persistent problem in poultry production. Nutritional deficiency is a basic factor associated with the condition. Even when nutrients are provided in slight excesses, lameness may continue because several factors can affect nutrient availability, absorption and utilization.

Factors associated with lameness

Factors may include poor-quality vitamin-mineral mixes, improper mixing, deficiency of a nutrient or its precursor, anticoccidial and antibacterial administration, improper pelleting or extrusion, heat treatment, poor-quality fish meal, unbalanced diets and malabsorption.

The role of calcium and phosphorous

Calcium and phosphorous supplementation may help when lameness is caused by deficiency of these nutrients. However, lameness can also arise from other nutritional factors. Excess calcium may interfere with absorption of other vital divalent cations such as manganese and zinc.

Rationale of the composition

Neurolyte combines nutritional factors involved in neurotransmitter release, muscle contraction, nerve structure and maintenance of the resting membrane potential.

Thiamine Supports acetylcholine release from presynaptic neurons.
Calcium Supports acetylcholine release and skeletal muscle contraction.
Phosphorous Supports phosphate buffering and acetylcholine receptor synthesis.
Vitamin D3 Supports calcium and phosphorous absorption and metabolism.
Riboflavin Supports nerve structure and myelination.
Choline Supports synthesis of the neurotransmitter acetylcholine.
Amino Nitrogen Supports development of postsynaptic acetylcholine receptors.
Zinc Supports neuromuscular activity and contraction mechanisms.
Manganese Supports neuromuscular activity.
Sodium, Potassium & Chloride Support resting membrane potential and normal activity of excitable cells.

Balanced neuromuscular transmission

Neurolyte supports neurotransmitter release from the presynaptic neuron, the action of acetylcholine at the motor endplate and the synthesis and development of acetylcholine receptors. Together, these functions support balanced transmission across the neuromuscular junction.

Product information

Composition

Thiamine, Choline, Zinc, Manganese, Riboflavin, Vitamin D3, Amino nitrogen (Peptide moiety), Sodium Citrate, Potassium Chloride with Organic Calcium and Phosphorous in an energy rich base.

Indications / Uses

  • To reduce the incidence and severity of lameness due to nutritional deficiency.
  • To reduce the incidence and severity of after-effects of Ranikhet disease (RD/ND).
  • To reduce the incidence and severity of vaccination reactions.

Usage Instructions

During the first three days of chick's life: 1 ml per litre of water.

To reduce severity of lameness: 1–2 ml per litre of water.

Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

5 litre and 1 litre bottles.
