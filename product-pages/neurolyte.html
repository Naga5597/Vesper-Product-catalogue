Product Profile

Neurolyte is a combination of selected vitamins and electrolytes (microminerals), along with amino nitrogen, formulated to address the root causes associated with lameness.

The formulation is designed to support neurotransmitter release, acetylcholine activity at the motor endplate and balanced transmission across the neuromuscular junction.

Key Ingredients

  • Thiamine
  • Choline
  • Zinc
  • Manganese
  • Riboflavin
  • Vitamin D3
  • Amino nitrogen (peptide moiety)
  • Sodium citrate
  • Potassium chloride
  • Organic calcium and phosphorous

Indications

  • Lameness due to nutritional deficiency
  • After-effects of Ranikhet disease (RD/ND)
  • Vaccination reactions

Usage Instructions

During the first three days of chick's life
1 ml per litre of water
To reduce the severity of lameness
1–2 ml per litre of water
Veterinary recommendation
Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

500 ml and 1 litre bottles.
