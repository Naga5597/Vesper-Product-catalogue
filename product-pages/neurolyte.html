Product information and technical overview.

Neuromuscular transmission in poultry

Effective neuromuscular transmission is essential for coordinated movement and normal muscular activity in poultry. Neurolyte provides nutritional support for the factors involved in timely message transmission and effective neuromuscular function.

The formulation combines selected vitamins, electrolytes (microminerals) and amino nitrogen to support the nutritional requirements associated with neuromuscular transmission.

History of lameness in birds

Lameness has been a persistent problem in poultry production. Nutritional deficiency is an important underlying factor. The condition may continue even when nutrients are provided in slight excesses because several factors can influence nutritional availability and utilization.

Factors associated with lameness include poor-quality vitamin-mineral mixes, improper mixing, deficiency of a nutrient or its precursor, anticoccidial and antibacterial administration, improper pelleting or extrusion, heat treatment, poor-quality fish meal, unbalanced diets and malabsorption.

Factors involved in lameness

Lameness should not be approached only through calcium, phosphorous and vitamin D3 supplementation. While these nutrients are important when deficient, other nutritional factors can also influence neuromuscular function.

Excess calcium may also interfere with the absorption of other vital divalent cations such as manganese and zinc. A balanced nutritional approach is therefore important when addressing neuromuscular problems in poultry.

Rationale of the composition

Neurolyte combines nutritional factors involved in neurotransmitter activity, muscle contraction, nerve structure and maintenance of membrane potential.

Thiamine

Supports acetylcholine release from the presynaptic neuron.

Calcium

Supports acetylcholine release and the contraction process in skeletal muscle.

Phosphorous

Acts as a phosphate buffer and supports mechanisms associated with acetylcholine receptor synthesis.

Vitamin D3

Supports proper absorption and metabolism of calcium and phosphorous.

Riboflavin

Supports axial cylinder strength and myelination of nerves.

Choline

Supports synthesis of the neurotransmitter acetylcholine in motoneurons.

Amino Nitrogen

Supports synthesis and development of postsynaptic acetylcholine receptors at the motor endplate.

Zinc and Manganese

Support mechanisms involved in neuromuscular activity and transmission.

Sodium, Potassium and Chloride

Help maintain the resting membrane potential of excitable cells.

Balanced neuromuscular transmission

  • Supports neurotransmitter release from the presynaptic neuron.
  • Supports the action of acetylcholine at the motor endplate.
  • Supports synthesis and development of acetylcholine receptors at the motor endplate.
  • Supports balanced transmission across the neuromuscular junction.

Product information

Composition

Thiamine, Choline, Zinc, Manganese, Riboflavin, Vitamin D3, Amino nitrogen (Peptide moiety), Sodium Citrate, Potassium Chloride with Organic Calcium and Phosphorous in an energy rich base.

Indications / Uses

  • To reduce the incidence and severity of lameness due to nutritional deficiency.
  • To reduce the severity of after-effects of Ranikhet disease (RD/ND).
  • To reduce the incidence and severity of vaccination reactions.

Usage Instructions

During the first three days of chick's life: 1 ml per litre of water.

To reduce the severity of lameness: 1–2 ml per litre of water.

Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

5 litre and 1 litre bottles.
