THERMOCARE +C

Beat the Heat — A combination of Electrolytes, Vitamins & Probiotics

Product information and technical overview.

Product overview

Helps birds adapt to rising temperature and discomfort of the summer months; relieves stress, provides instant energy, corrects electrolyte imbalance, reduces high corticosterone levels, lowers mortality rate, improves feed intake and feed conversion.

Formulation concept

THERMOCARE +C contains electrolytes, buffer acids, Vitamin C, sodium salicylate, polysaccharides and Lactobacillus, improved with ammonium ion and nitrogen moiety to take care of physiological variations during stress, especially heat stress and brooding temperature variations. It also improves nutrient availability by virtue of improved digestion, absorption and metabolism made possible by the probiotic and instantly available energy.

Heat stress in poultry birds

All types of birds are sensitive to heat stress. Hot weather management and hot climate control are crucial. Prevention and management measures include evaporative cooling, increasing air velocity at bird level to 2–3 m/sec, insulation of water tanks and pipes, roof water sprinklers, cool drinking water, avoiding wet litter, and appropriate dietary and energy management.

Temperature and physiology

65–80°F: desirable temperature under which chicken feels comfortable and functions normally. 81–95°F: feed consumption drops; for each degree rise in temperature, 2–3% loss of feed intake is stated. 96–100°F: severe heat stress range. 101°F and above: potentially lethal temperature range. Panting increases respiratory rate, decreases blood carbon dioxide and produces respiratory alkalosis; heat stress also depletes potassium and other minerals, altering electrolyte balance.

Product information

Composition

Electrolytes, Vitamin C, Lactobacillus, Sodium-bi-Carbonate, Sodium Salicylate, fortified with Polysaccharides in instant Energy Base.

Indications / Uses

  • Stresses of all type including heat stress.

Usage Instructions

Feed — Regular Use: 250–500 gms/ton of feed. During Stress: 1 kg/ton of feed.
Water Soluble Powder — Regular Use: 1 gm in 5 Liters of drinking water; During Stress: 1 gm in 2 Liters of drinking water.
Liquid: 1 ml/1–2 Ltr of drinking water. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian. Note: Do not use feed grade material in drinking water.

Presentation

Water Soluble Powder: 500 gm tin and 1 kg × 20 nos per bag.
