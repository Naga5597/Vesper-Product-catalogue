VESMIN FORTE

Chelated Trace Minerals with Probiotics and Saccharomyces Yeast

Product information and technical overview.

Product concept

VESMIN FORTE is a chelated trace mineral premix for poultry containing essential trace minerals in mineral proteinate form to improve bioavailability. It contains manganese, zinc, copper, cobalt, iron, iodine, chromium and selenium.

Chelated mineral absorption

A trace mineral must become attached to an organic molecule or escort before absorption. Proteinated trace minerals follow a different absorption pathway from normal trace minerals, reducing competition for the same uptake mechanism. Intestinal absorption is increased by about 50% for proteinated trace minerals.

Advantages

Chelated trace minerals are described as soluble in intestinal contents, stable in the intestinal tract, protected from antagonists and gut pH, more readily absorbed than inorganic forms, and better utilized metabolically by reducing interactions and sequestration.

Product information

Composition

Each kg contains: Manganese 80 gm, Zinc 80 gm, Copper 15 gm, Cobalt 0.5 gm, Iron 60 gm, Iodine 1 gm, Chromium 12.5 mg, Selenium 0.3 gm, Lactobacillus 1.5 million spores, Saccharomyces Cerevisiae 10 × 10^? CFU, Amino Nitrogen q.s. in fortified base. ()

Indications / Uses

  • Replaces inorganic trace minerals in feed.
  • Provides chelated trace minerals protected from adverse reactions of other nutrients.
  • Prevents competition for absorption among minerals.
  • Provides an instant mineral source during stress and deficiency.
  • Supports health, production and reproduction.
  • Reduces incidences of leg weakness and broken eggs.

Usage Instructions

750 gm to 1 kg per ton of feed. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

20 kg bag.
