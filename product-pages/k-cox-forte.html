K-COX FORTE

Vitamin K support for bleeding and coagulation

Product information and technical overview.

Vitamin K and blood clotting

Vitamin K helps in the blood-clotting process. Vitamin K deficiency increases coagulation/clotting time and can cause profuse bleeding, shock and death. Vitamin K is involved in the synthesis and activation of prothrombin and factors VII, IX and X in the liver.

Coccidiosis context

Coccidiosis can destroy intestinal cells and cause hemorrhages. Oocyst development and persistence contribute to intestinal damage and bleeding.

Vitamin K forms for feed use

Vitamin K1 is not used by the feed industry because of cost and lack of a stabilized form. Water-soluble Vitamin K3/menadione salts include MSBC, MSB and MPB, with menadione concentrations of 33.0%, 50.0% and 45.4% respectively.

Debeaking

Debeaking/beak trimming can cause hemorrhage or bleeding, particularly in layers and breeders.

Product information

Composition

Each 50 gm contains: Vitamin K 500 mg; Carriers q.s.

Indications / Uses

  • During debeaking and outbreak of Coccidiosis.
  • To prevent Vitamin K deficiency syndrome in poultry, e.g. prolonged bleeding, large hematoma and severe hemorrhagic anemia.
  • To prevent hemorrhage in case of coccidiosis, necrotic enteritis, ulceration etc.
  • After treatment with sulpha drugs.

Usage Instructions

Chick & Growers: 50 gm for 3000 birds in drinking water for 5–7 days. During Coccidiosis: 1 gm in 5 liters of drinking water for 7–10 days along with treatment. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

50 gm pouch.
