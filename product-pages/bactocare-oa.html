Product information and technical overview.

Organic acids for health and performance

Organic acids are used globally by the feed industry for management of gut microbiology, improved performance and health. Their use supports better nutrient utilization and the activity of enzymes such as phytase, better body weights, improved feed conversion and reduced colonization of the gut by pathogens, helping reduce disease and disturbance in the flock.

Further context

Propionic acid has been used for more than 30 years to reduce bacterial growth and mould in feedstuffs. Formic, fumaric, citric and lactic acids and their salts are associated with effects on feed conversion, daily gain, diarrhea incidence, feed cost and time to market.

Organic acid definition

Organic acids are a broad class of compounds used in fundamental metabolic processes. They share water solubility, acidity and ninhydrin-negativity and generally include carboxylic acids with or without keto or hydroxyl groups, while excluding most amino acids.

Post-table technical note

Amino nitrogen and reactive protein stimulate macrophage activation and improve cell-mediated immunity in the birds. LPO (Lactoperoxidase) is bacteriostatic and bactericidal, so it supports probiotics. Bactocare OA+ provides a maximized comprehensive probiotic activity to competitively inhibit, antagonize and exclude pathogens for improved production and profit, thereby reducing dependence on AGP (Antibiotic Growth Promoters).

Product information

Composition

ORGANIC ACIDS (Formic acid, Sorbic acid, Propionic Acid, Acetic acid, Ascorbic acid and Lactic acid), Ammonium propionate, Ammonium Formate along with fatty acids, yeast extract, Amino Nitrogen, LPO, reactive Proteins in a fortified base.

Indications / Uses

  • Reduces the gut pH.
  • Reduces multiplication of pathogenic gram-negative microorganisms and fungus.
  • Increases tissue response and improves nutrient utilization.
  • Reduces disease incidences.
  • Bactocare OA+ provides an alternative to antibiotic growth promoters.
  • Reduces duration and cost of treatment.

Usage Instructions

Regular Use: 250 gm–500 gm/ton of feed. During Stress: 2 kg/ton of feed. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

1 kg × 25 packets in a bag.
