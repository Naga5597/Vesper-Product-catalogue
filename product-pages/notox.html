NOTOX

Specific Action — Toxin Destroyer

Product information and technical overview.

Mycotoxins

Mycotoxins are toxic metabolites produced by filamentous fungi and moulds, including Aspergillus, Fusarium and Penicillium. Important groups include aflatoxins, zearalenones, ochratoxin A, trichothecenes, deoxynivalenol, fumonisins and T2 toxins, with effects on the liver, kidneys, nervous system, production, fertility, hatchability and immunity.

Specific action

NOTOX is a formulation based on biotransformation of toxins and toxic metabolites. It alters the molecular structure of preformed mycotoxins into non-toxic metabolites and also acts against endotoxins present in feed.

In-vitro aflatoxin studies

In-vitro studies evaluated feed containing 375 ppb and 1000 ppb Aflatoxin B1 with Notox levels of 0.025%, 0.050% and 0.100% over 24, 48 and 72 hours. The studies recorded analyzable Aflatoxin B1 values and calculated detoxification percentages at an experimental pH of approximately 6.2–6.5.

Comparison with binders

Aluminosilicates/zeolites and mannan oligosaccharide are commonly used toxin binders, but non-specific binding can affect nutrient availability. NOTOX acts as an aflatoxin B1 destroyer, altering its molecular structure into non-toxic metabolites and then binding the altered molecules. Its stabilized protein moiety is specific in action and stable under gastrointestinal conditions.

Product information

Composition

NOTOX contains Bio active protein moiety in fortified base.

Indications / Uses

  • Specific-action mycotoxin and endotoxin destroyer.
  • NOTOX remains stable in the GI tract and helps prevent immunosuppression and disease outbreaks in layers.
  • NOTOX improves weight gain and FCR in broilers and supports fertility, hatchability and chick numbers in breeders.

Usage Instructions

150 gms to 250 gms per ton of feed, or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

25 × 1 kg in container.
