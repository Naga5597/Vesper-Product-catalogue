Composition / Contents

NOTOX NOTOX contains Bioactive protein moiety in fortified base. Mycotoxins are toxic metabolites produced by filamentous fungi and moulds of three major genera of fungus like Aspergillus, Fusarium and Pencillium. Resultant toxic metabolites are Aflatoxins, Zearalenones (ZON), Ochratoxin A (OTA), Trichothecenes, Deoxyvalenol (DON), Fumenesis (FUM) and T2 toxins which invariably affect the chicken leading to mild to severe Hepatotoxicity, Nephrotoxicity, Neurotoxicity, production slumps, poor fertlity, hatchability and immunosuppression. Uneven flock size, fatty liver conditions and poor response to vaccinationand subsequent disease outbreaks are the most common problems suffered by the poultry farmers and breeders. Mould inhibitors, toxin binders and Acidifiers are not really beneficial to solve the problem efficiently. Already performed Mycotoxins and their illeffects on the vital organs like liver, and kidney's will not be contained by toxin binders. USAGE INSTRUCTIONS: SPECIFIC ACTION Looking into these complications, VESPER PHARMACEUTICALS have introduced an unique formulation NOTOX with the new concept of biotransformation of the toxins and their toxic metabolites after lots of in house and field trials. NOTOX is a Mycotoxin destroyer that alters the molecular structure of performed MYCOTOXINS into non toxic metabolites. NOTOX also destroys ENDOTOXINS present in the feed.
