The respiratory regulatory centers in the brain regulate the amount of carbon dioxide that is exhaled by controlling the speed and depth of breathing, When breathing is increased blood Carbon Dioxide (C02) leveldecreases and blood becomes more basic.

When breathing is decreased blood Carbon Dioxide (Co2) level increases and blood becomes more acidic. By adjusting the speed and depth of breathing the respiratory regulatory centers and lungs are able to regulate the blood pH minute by minute. CYTOKINESTORM An abnormality in this control mechanism of pH can cause one ofthe major disturbances in acid - base balance. i.e. acidosis or alkalosis. These conditions are not diseases but they are the result of a variety of disorders and disturbances. Metabolic acidosis and metabolic alkalosisare caused by an imbalancein theproduction and excretionof acids or bases by the kidneys.

Respiratory acidosis or alkalosis are primarily caused by lung and breathing disorders. Kidneys also try to compensate by excreting more acid in the urine.
