AMICHOL

Optimizes Choline Utilization

Product information and technical overview.

Choline variability

Choline occurs naturally in all ingredients and there is no consistency in choline content in natural feedstuffs. Bioavailability is highly variable and variation may occur due to crop growth conditions, soil and location.

Role of choline

The main function of choline is to act as a lipotropic agent and prevent abnormal fatty infiltration in liver (Fatty Liver syndrome), ensuring proper metabolism and effective utilization of nutrients. It also helps in formation of acetylcholine for proper functioning of the nervous system. Oviduct contraction and emptying of the crop are also stimulated by release of acetylcholine.

Methyl-group metabolism

Choline serves as a methyl donor after conversion to betaine, providing labile methyl groups for formation of methionine from homocystine and creatine from guanidoacetic acid. Choline therefore spares dietary methionine for other metabolic functions.

Why utilization matters

Choline must be converted to betaine before it can donate methyl groups. Uptake and conversion can be adversely affected by feed components, gut infection, malabsorption, liver problems, low intramitochondrial organic calcium/inorganic phosphorus, phospholipase limitations and ionophores/EDTA. Improving choline availability is therefore important rather than simply increasing choline chloride.

How Amichol works

The specific polysaccharide moiety in Amichol triggers activity of Phospholipase A2 from the pancreas and mitochondrial membranes, improving transmembrane transport of choline and conversion into betaine. Phospholipid supports absorption from the gut; organic calcium and inorganic phosphorus assist the mitochondrial microenvironment; Vitamin B6 and sulphate radicals optimize methionine utilization; Lactobacillus reduces ammonia production and its adverse effects. The combined effect optimizes methyl-group management and helps meet choline requirements at a lower level of choline.

Product information

Composition

Polysaccharide 5000 ppm, Inorganic Phosphorous 5000 ppm, Choline 20% with Organic Calcium, Folic Acid, Pyridoxine, Sulphate Radicals, Lactic Acid Bacillus and Phospholipids in a fortified base.

Indications / Uses

  • For better utilization of Feed.
  • For better utilization of Methionine.
  • For improved growth, Production and FCR.
  • For better liver health and performance.

Usage Instructions

500 gm to 1 kg per ton of feed. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation
