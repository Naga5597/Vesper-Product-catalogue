ENDOMETH

Look beyond synthetic methionine

Technical information reproduced from the supplied Vesper brochure in a digital, scrollable format.

Enterokinase and methionine reconversion

The brochure focuses on two mechanisms: enterokinase stimulation and methionine reconversion. It explains that methionine is the first limiting amino acid in poultry diets and that other amino acids are utilized only to the extent of available methionine.

Supplementing synthetic methionine

The brochure notes that calculated methionine from feed ingredients may vary with ingredient quality. Even when synthetic methionine is supplemented, there may still be a deficit if the expected dietary contribution is not realized.

Maximizing methionine utilization

Supplying protease may help only to a certain extent. The brochure describes eliciting a greater enterokinase response to food as a way to improve native protease activity. Maximizing conversion of trypsinogen to trypsin is described as improving protein digestibility and reducing the requirement for trypsinogen production, which has a high sulphur-amino-acid content.

Enhanced methionine reconversion

When the body requires methyl groups, methionine is converted to homocystine. Homocystine can be reconverted to methionine in the presence of a labile methyl donor, but the brochure notes that this may not occur efficiently due to inadequate activation of the enzyme system. Aminonitrogen in Endometh is stated to activate the corresponding enzyme system and promote reconversion of homocystine into methionine.

Broiler trial reported in the brochure

A commercial broiler trial used 580 day-old chicks divided into control and Endometh groups for 42 days. The brochure reports weekly body weight and feed intake, FCR calculation, and antibody titre testing. It reports average body weight 5 g higher in the Endometh group, 153 g less feed consumed per bird, FCR of 2.02, similar immunity, and an additional return of Rs. 1260 per 1000 broilers compared with DL-methionine under the stated trial assumptions. The brochure also gives its stated DL-methionine and Endometh price assumptions and feed-cost calculations.

Product information

Composition

Methyl donors and Aminonitrogen in a base enriched with Natural enhancers of Enterokinase Activity.

Indications / Uses

  • To enhance the methionine value of feedstuffs.

Usage Instructions

In synthetic methionine supplemented diets: replace total synthetic methionine content with equal quantity of Endometh. In non-supplemented diets: add 100–150 gm of Endometh for better feed utilization, productivity and FCR.

Presentation

20 Kg bags.
