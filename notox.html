NOTOX

Specific Action — Toxin Destroyer

Technical information reproduced from the supplied Vesper brochure in a digital, scrollable format.

Mycotoxins

The brochure describes mycotoxins as toxic metabolites produced by filamentous fungi and moulds, including Aspergillus, Fusarium and Penicillium. It lists aflatoxins, zearalenones, ochratoxin A, trichothecenes, deoxynivalenol, fumonisins and T2 toxins and discusses their effects on liver, kidney, nervous system, production, fertility, hatchability and immunity.

Specific action

NOTOX is described as a unique formulation based on biotransformation of toxins and toxic metabolites. The brochure states that NOTOX alters the molecular structure of preformed mycotoxins into non-toxic metabolites and also destroys endotoxins present in feed.

In-vitro aflatoxin studies

The supplied brochure reports in-vitro studies using feed containing 375 ppb and 1000 ppb Aflatoxin B1, with Notox levels of 0.025%, 0.050% and 0.100% and observations at 24, 48 and 72 hours. It reports analyzable Aflatoxin B1 values and calculated detoxification percentages, with experimental pH around 6.2–6.5. These are brochure-reported in-vitro results.

Comparison with binders

The brochure discusses aluminosilicates/zeolites and mannan oligosaccharide as commonly used toxin binders and argues that non-specific binding can affect nutrient availability. It presents Notox as an aflatoxin B1 destroyer that alters molecular structure to non-toxic metabolites and then binds altered molecules, with a stabilized protein moiety described as specific in action and stable under gastrointestinal conditions.

Product information

Composition

NOTOX contains Bio active protein moiety in fortified base.

Indications / Uses

  • Specific-action mycotoxin and endotoxin destroyer.
  • The brochure states that it is stable in the GI tract and prevents immunosuppression and disease outbreaks in layers.
  • The brochure states that Notox improves weight gain and FCR in broilers and fertility, hatchability and number of chicks in breeders.

Usage Instructions

150 gms to 250 gms per ton of feed, or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

25 × 1 kg in container.
