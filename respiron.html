RESPIRON

Inspires Respiration

Technical information reproduced from the supplied Vesper brochure in a digital, scrollable format.

Respiratory acid-base balance

The respiratory regulatory centers control carbon dioxide exhalation through speed and depth of breathing. Increased breathing decreases blood CO2 and makes blood more basic; decreased breathing increases CO2 and makes blood more acidic. The brochure discusses acidosis and alkalosis and the compensatory role of kidneys.

Respiratory distress

During respiratory distress such as Coryza, CRD and RDC, mucus can accumulate in alveolar spaces, reducing breathing depth and gaseous exchange. The brochure describes resulting respiratory acidosis and associated distress, drowsiness, poor resistance, low productivity and poor performance.

Cytokine storm and pulmonary surfactant

The brochure describes a cytokine storm as an exaggerated immune response involving inflammatory mediators. It states that respiratory infection can damage alveoli and lung tissue. Pulmonary surfactant is described as a mixture of lipids and surfactant-specific proteins covering the alveolar surface and reducing surface tension.

Respiron concept

Respiron is described as modifying the biophysical behaviour of the lungs. The brochure states that its immunopotentiating properties support adaptive immunity of the lungs, that its surfactant acts as a first-line defense against invading organisms, and that Respiron improves bronchial clearance through mucolytic action.

Antibiotic-response claim in brochure

The brochure states that an antibiotic course of 5–7 days is costly and may have undesirable effects on feed intake and productivity, and claims that Respiron can improve response to antibiotics and reduce the course to 3–4 days. This is reproduced as a claim from the supplied brochure, not independently verified.

Product information

Composition

Alkalizers, Urinary Acidifiers, buffers and Amino nitrogen in fortified base.

Indications / Uses

  • The brochure positions Respiron for respiratory distress and susceptible flocks.

Usage Instructions

Feed premix: 500 gms–1 kg per ton of feed or as recommended. During respiratory distress: 1 ml per liter of water for 4 days with prescribed antibiotic; continue Respiron for 3 days after antibiotic. In susceptible flocks as a precautionary measure: 0.5–1 ml per liter of water. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

Feed premix: 1 kg × 25 drum. Liquid packing: 1 Ltr., 5 Ltr. & 30 Ltrs.
