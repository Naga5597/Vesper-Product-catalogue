  Mycotoxins are toxic metabolites produced by filamentous fungi and moulds of three
  major genera of fungus like Aspergillus, Fusarium and Pencillium. Resultant toxic
  metabolites are Aflatoxins, Zearalenones (ZON), Ochratoxin A (OTA), Trichothecenes,
  Deoxyvalenol (DON), Fumenesis (FUM) and T2 toxins which invariably affect the chicken
 leading to mild to severe Hepatotoxicity, Nephrotoxicity, Neurotoxicity, production slumps,
 poor fertlity, hatchability and immunosuppression. Uneven flock size, fatty liver conditions
 and poor response to vaccinationand subsequent disease outbreaks are the most common
 problems suffered by the poultry farmers and breeders. Mould inhibitors, toxin binders and
 Acidifiers are not really beneficial to solve the problem efficiently. Already performed
 Mycotoxins and their ill effects on the vital organs like liver, and kidney's will not
 be contained by toxin binders.
 Looking into these complications, VESPER PHARMACEUTICALS have introduced an
 unique formulation NOTOX with the new concept of biotransformation of the toxins and
 their toxic metabolites after lots of in house and field trials. NOTOX is a Mycotoxin
 destroyer that alters the molecular structure of performed MYCOTOXINS into non toxic
 metabolites. NOTOX also destroys ENDOTOXINS present in the feed.

  Micotoxin contamination of feedstuffs/finished feeds is one of the serious
  problems faced by poultry farmers in the Country. Methods followed to reduce the
  severity of this problem aim at reducing the fungal growth in feedstuffs or binding
 the toxins or acombination of both. Though antifungal check the growth of fungi.
 they can not take care of preformed toxins. Hence the preferred choice is toxin
  binders while some farmers add antifungal aslo with toxin binders.
 The two widely used toxin binders are aluminosilicates (Zeolite) and mannan
 oligosaccharide(mos). The use of aluminosilicates is quite common in poultry
 farms in India. Even though the toxin binding ability of aluminosilicates is high as
 shown in some in vitro trials, being a non-specific binder it may bind any one or more
 of the important vitamins/minerals/amino acids. Such a non-specific binding would
 reduce both the nutrient availability as well as the aflatoxin binding ablilty of
 zeolite. On the other hand, mannan oligosaccharide is shown to bind to the cell wall
projections of bacteria and hence it's activity also may be reduced to an extent,
depending upon the bacterial load.
Thus, the actual Micotoxin binding ability of mannan oligosaccharide of zeolites
may be greatly reduced in the intestines, where the binding action is very
essential. And apart from simple adsorption, these compounds seem to have little
role in reducing the aflatoxin levels by other mechanisms. Hence, the reduction of
free toxin levels to a safe and harmless levels is not assured.

Notox, developed at Vesper Pharmaceuticals is a product with a new concept to
fight toxins. Notox is an aflatoxindestroyer that alters the molecular structure of
aflatoxin B1 (the most harmful type of aflatoxin for poultry) in to non-toxic
metabolites like aflatoxicol and aflatoxin B2b and then binds on to the altered
molecules., A stabilized protein moiety, Notox is specific in action, stable under the
gastrointestinal conditions. Notox does not react with any other
chemicals/molecules. Notox doubly assures the safety of your birds.
