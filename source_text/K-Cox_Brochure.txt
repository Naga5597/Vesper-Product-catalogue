Vitamin K is required for syntheses of prothrombin (factor Il) plasma cloting factor VIl (Proconvertin)
christmas factor (X) and stuart-powerfactor (X), are all synthesized in the liver as inactive precursors forms,
later conjugated to biologically active proteins by the action of Vitamin K to prevent hemorrhage.
Vitamin K starts to work in approx. 6 hours time after its consumption.

Subclinical and Clinical Coccidiosis.
Different Stages of coccidia in chickens apper both within the host as well as outside. The developmental stages
in the chicken give rise to a microscopic egg (called an oocyst) that is passed out in the droppings. Under proper
conditions of temperature and moisture, the oocyst develops within two days, to form a sporulated oocyst, which
is capable of infecting other chickens. At this stage, the oocyst contains eight bodies (called sporozoites),each of
which is capable of entering a cell in the chicken's intestine after the oocyst is eaten. when sporozoites enter the
cells, they divide many times producing either a few or many offspring (merozoites).
The numbers produced depend on the species of coccidia involved. Each merozoite,in turn, may enter another
intestinal cell.

This cycle may be repeated several times, because of this cyclic multiplication, large numbers of intestinal cells
are destroyed causing hemorrhages. Oocysts remain viable in litter for months. In this way, they can contaminate
a farm from year to year. Oocysts are killed by freezing, extreme dryness, high temperatures and by use of
effective drugs.

Debeaking Causes Hemorrhage/Bleeding
Debeaking, also called beak trimming, is the patial removal of the beak of poultry, especially layer hens and
breeders although it may also be performed on quail, turkeys and ducks. Most commonly, the beak is
shortened  permanently, although regrowth can occur,. Beak trimming is most common in egg-laying strains
of chickens.
