Metabolically, liver is the most active organ of the chicken's body. A major portion of body's total metabolic
reactions, beit assimilation of nutrients, catabolism of drugs or detoxification of toxins, take place in liver.
For this purpose, liver cells are packed with a variety of enzymes that catalyze different reactions. While
kidneys are the main organ for Excretion of waste and metabolised products.
Unfortunately, most liver disorders result in fat accumulation in liver. Depending on the degree of
accumulation, the capacity of liver cells to perform the regular metabolic functions goes down, affecting
growth, production and detoxification processes. At later stages, the condition also affects kidneys due to
increased toxicity. More importantly, the clinical signs will be seen only after about 75 % of the liver is
damaged. Lipo tropic agents may not be of much help due to their insignificant contribution towards the
transport of lipids from liver cells to outside. What is needed in this situation is a product that accelerates the
rate of removal of lipid molecules from liver cells.
