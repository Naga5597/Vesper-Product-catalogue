ZYMOLACT

New Generation Performance Enhancer

Technical information reproduced from the supplied Vesper brochure in a digital, scrollable format.

Four program options

The brochure presents four options: savings in feed input; savings in feed cost; savings in DLM/Choline; and savings in liver tonics. It states that Zymolact can be used in different ways and recommends selecting a suitable program or consulting a veterinarian/nutritionist before altering feed formulation.

What makes Zymolact superior?

Zymolact is described as developed to address batchmate non-uniformity and limitations of various performance promoters. It integrates working principles of different performance promoters. Unique Gut Stabilization Factors (GSF), Lumen Trophic Factors (LTF) and Zymogen Inducers (ZI) are described as maximizing expression and activation of native gut enzymes for better substrate degradation.

Digestion and gut environment

The brochure states that exogenous enzymes complement host enzymes to improve digestion and reduce adverse effects of antinutritional factors. GSF and LTF are described as acting synergistically to create a gut microenvironment with a large absorptive surface and optimum pH, maximizing nutrient absorption. Derivatives of LTF are described as supporting natural somatotrophic hormone activity and nutrient retention/utilization.

Additional components

The brochure describes probiotic cultures, methyl donors with a sparing effect on methionine and/or choline chloride, and a lipotrophic effect supporting liver health and performance.

Batch uniformity

The brochure states that a proportion of birds in broiler/layer batches may perform below optimum, consume relatively more feed and contribute disproportionately to production losses, especially during stress. It contrasts single-purpose performance promoters with Zymolact’s broader approach.

Product information

Composition

Probiotics (Bacillus spp., Lactic Acid Bacillus, Saccharomyces Yeast), Enzymes (Amylase, Protease, Cellulase, β-galactosidase, Lipase, Phytase) with Gut Stabilizers, Lumen Trophic Factors, Zymogen Inducers, Methyl Donors and Aminonitrogen (Stress Proteins derived from various sources) in a fortified base.

Indications / Uses

  • To improve, maintain and enhance efficiency of gut for digestion, absorption and assimilation of nutrients.
  • Improves production and FCR even during stress.

Usage Instructions

500 g–1 kg per ton of feed regularly, or as directed by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

5 & 20 Kg bags.
