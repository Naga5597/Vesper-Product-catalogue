TOXOL

Nutritional Liver & Kidney Supplement

Technical information reproduced from the supplied Vesper brochure in a digital, scrollable format.

Liver and kidney role

The brochure describes the liver as the most metabolically active organ of the chicken and the site of many nutrient-assimilation, drug-catabolism and toxin-detoxification reactions. Kidneys are described as the main organs for excretion of waste and metabolized products.

Fat accumulation and metabolic stress

The brochure states that many liver disorders result in fat accumulation and that increasing accumulation reduces liver-cell metabolic functions, affecting growth, production and detoxification. It notes that clinical signs may appear only after about 75% of the liver is damaged and presents a need to accelerate removal of lipid molecules from liver cells.

Lipid metabolism and Toxol

The brochure describes lipotropic factors, lipo-acceptor protein, lipoprotein lipase, triglycerides, phospholipids, sphingolipids, ketone bodies, cholesterol and steroids in its explanation of lipid metabolism and the unique action of Toxol.

Commercial broiler data reported in brochure

A 36-day commercial broiler comparison is presented with control and Toxol treatment groups. The brochure states that body weights increased with Toxol dosage, FCR improved compared with control and mortality was reduced in treatment groups. The displayed data include body-weight/FCR/mortality/feed-consumption values; these are reproduced as brochure-reported trial data.

Product information

Composition

Toxol Feed Premix: Choline Chloride, Methionine, Inositol, Vitamin B12, base fortified with Predigested Protein and Aminonitrogen. Toxol Liquid: each 10 ml contains Tricholine Citrate 100 mg, Methionine 10 mg, Liver extract 50 mg, Protein Hydrolysate 50 mg, Inositol 2 mg, Vitamin B12 0.33 mcg, base fortified with Predigested Protein and Aminonitrogen.

Indications / Uses

  • Improves Liver & Kidney Health.
  • Protect from damaging effect of Mycotoxins.
  • Improves Nutrient Utilization.

Usage Instructions

Toxol Feed: Normal Mix — 500 gms to 1 kg per ton of feed. Under Stress/Mycotoxins — add 2 to 2.5 kgs per ton of feed. Toxol Liquid: 5–15 ml/100 birds. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

Toxol Feed Premix: 1 kg, 5 kg, 10 kg, 20 kg bags. Toxol Liquid: 500 ml, 1 Liter, 5 Liter and 30 Liter Drum.
