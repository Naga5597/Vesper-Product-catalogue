PROTOSOL

Liquid Feed Supplement

Technical information reproduced from the supplied Vesper brochure in a digital, scrollable format.

Why amino acids

Various protein sources such as fish and soya contain different amino-acid profiles that vary in proportions and may be unpredictable. Amino acids are described as pre-digested proteins in simpler forms for immediate and quick absorption.

Advantages

PROTOSOL is described as a combination of essential and non-essential amino acids with certain B-group vitamins in a fortified base, intended to be complementary for optimum performance.

Product information

Composition

Each 5 ml contains: Pyridoxine HCl 4 mg, Niacinamide 15 mg, Cyanocobalamine 15 mcg, Protein Hydrolysate 50 mg, Selenium 2 ppm, Biotin 2 ppm, Amino acids 4.21% (Arginine 0.9%, Tryptophan 0.11%, Phenylalanine 0.7%, Glycine 1.1%, Lysine 0.8%).

Indications / Uses

  • Excellent growth and feed conversion in chicks and broilers.
  • Stimulates and maintains egg production when feed consumption goes down.
  • Regular supply of amino acids even when protein content of fish meal and GNC is uncertain.
  • Overcomes post-mycotoxin problems and maintains production in suspected protein-deficient diets.
  • Reduces convalescence period and hence faster recovery.
  • Helps tissue construction and repair.
  • Stimulates and maintains production in later half of laying cycle.

Usage Instructions

Chicks: 5 ml/100 birds. Breeders: 10 ml/100 birds. Broilers & Growers: 15–20 ml/100 birds. Layers: 25–30 ml/100 birds. For 5–7 days or as recommended.

Presentation

1 Ltr bottle, 5 Ltr jar & 30 Ltr drum.
