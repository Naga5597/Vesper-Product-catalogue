NEPHROCARE

Renal support for poultry

Technical information reproduced from the supplied Vesper brochure in a digital, scrollable format.

Avian urinary system

The urinary system filters, secretes, absorbs, stores and eliminates urine. The brochure describes paired trilobed kidneys, ureters, renal lobules, cortex and medulla, and the nephron as the functional unit. It describes reptilian-type and mammalian-type nephrons and the filtering/reabsorption process.

Gout in poultry

Gout is described as abnormal accumulation of urates in kidneys and other organs. The brochure distinguishes visceral and articular forms and lists possible contributing factors including calcium/phosphorus imbalance, high calcium diets in chicks/growers, urea adulteration, mycotoxins, high protein levels, poor-quality proteins, rock phosphate, bacterial/viral infections, zinc and heavy-metal toxicity, certain antibiotics and genetic problems.

Nephrocare concept

The brochure presents Nephrocare as containing nephromodulator substances, sodium fluoride, butyric acid and aminonitrogen in a fortified base. It describes sodium fluoride as a kidney stimulant, butyric acid as reducing chances of kidney damage by pathogens, and nephromodulator substances as supporting physiological renal functioning and rejuvenation.

Reported outcomes

The brochure states that improved renal function can support detoxification, endogenous Vitamin C generation, reabsorption of nutrients/electrolytes and haemopoiesis, and states that Nephrocare reduces mortality due to gout/ascites and assists renal-tissue rejuvenation.

Product information

Composition

Nephromodulators, Sodium Fluoride, Butyric Acid with Aminonitrogen in a fortified base.

Indications / Uses

  • To improve and stimulate renal functioning in the management of Gout, HPS and Ascites.

Usage Instructions

Liquid: Add 1 ml/Liter of drinking water. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian. Feed: 1 kg per ton of feed. Or as recommended by the Veterinarian.

Presentation

Liquid: 1 liter & 5 liters jar. Feed: 20 kg Bag.
